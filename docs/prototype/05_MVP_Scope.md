# Vinci — MVP scope
Version 1.0 · 1 November 2026

## Release definition
Demonstrable browser MVP plus hosted account/profile functionality. Suitable for product walkthroughs and early feedback; not for accepting real payments, running genuine livestreams, certifying ownership or promising deliveries.

## Included and functional
- Public browsing of eight sample disciplines, sample creators and streams.
- Hosted auth integration, session state and signed-in/out navigation.
- Owner profile reads/writes and role display.
- Public broadcaster setup form, browser-local Visitor Number and required fields.
- Search/filter/calculator interactions over sample data.
- Dedicated stream, Versus, Registry, Global, Cowrie and virtual-gallery views.

## Demonstrations only
Multi-camera feed, chat, reactions, auctions, marketplace purchases, gifts, commissions, paid votes, certificates, wallets, payout estimates, awards/XP/posts/events, language previews and faux-3D exhibitions. No demo transaction has financial or ownership effect.

## Known gaps
1. Start broadcast generates a nonexistent catalogue ID and reaches not-found.
2. Go Live disciplines do not match all eight Discover categories; taxonomy alignment is deferred.
3. ART region IDs in sample profiles are not assigned to registered users; VNC and certificate identities are not authoritative.
4. Client-only monetisation controls do not establish server permission.
5. No persistent money, orders, event registration or social content.
6. Historical schema migrations omit explicit grants; consolidated reference documents live ACLs.
7. Registry and other page copy sometimes describes capabilities beyond demo implementation.
8. No automated business-rule test suite or measured load/cost benchmark is supplied by the existing app.

## Launch boundary
Do not announce real broadcasting, paid admission, cash-out, blockchain minting, shipping or realtime audience interaction. Early access is a demonstration/account-profile trial. Commercial launch requires the deferred services, legal checks and operational tests described in the Launch Plan.

## Change control
Preserve the owner's decision to stay MVP. Future work requires an agreed phase, measurable acceptance checks and budget. Proposed schemas and fees in the handover are not deployed features or newly agreed commercial policies.
