# Vinci — Launch plan
Version 1.0 · 1 November 2026 · Relative phases, not committed dates

## Phase A — handover and internal review
Owner + delivery team review source, scope and known gaps. Configure the target environment securely; verify auth provider/confirmation/redirect settings. Recheck build, account creation, sign-in/out, profile save/read-back, privacy and public routes. Publish only on owner instruction; this handover does not republish the app.
Exit: demonstration works within declared limits and no sensitive data is exported.

## Phase B — invitation-only demonstration
Recruit a small opted-in set of creators/viewers across priority disciplines. Show sample content with explicit explanation that streaming, transactions, certificates and logistics are demonstrations. Collect consented feedback on discovery, profile layout, Cowrie comprehension and gallery interest. Use interviews rather than claiming uninstrumented conversion metrics.
Exit: documented feedback, owner-approved next priority and budget. No payment acceptance or fulfilment promises.

## Phase C — operational pilot (only if scope expands)
Priority sequence: repair persistent broadcaster lifecycle + managed video; enforce server identity/permissions; introduce moderated chat; integrate Cowrie ledger/payments; then orders/certificates and fulfilment; finally paid battles/galleries and full localisation.
Choose geography and payout rails with providers/legal advisers. Complete fraud, tax, privacy, consumer-rights, creator payout and stored-value reviews. Agree marketplace/admission fees, refunds, carrier charges, customs and dispute responsibilities.
Exit: end-to-end real-user tests, reconciled payments, accurate ledger, confirmed contract/certificates if offered, tested shipping and support processes.

## Phase D — limited commercial release
Approve written release criteria, monitor video cost and fraud exposure, publish controlled availability and supported regions. Maintain incident response, moderation and support staffing. Gradually increase viewer/event capacity only after observed load and cost tests.

## Proposed ownership
| Area | Responsible party to appoint |
|---|---|
| Scope, policies, go/no-go | Product owner |
| Auth, privacy, security | Engineering/security owner |
| Streaming and runtime health | Engineering/operations |
| Creator onboarding and moderation | Community operations |
| Money, payout reconciliation, tax | Finance/compliance |
| Gallery curation | Event coordinator |
| Packaging, carrier tracking, disputes | Fulfilment/support |

## Pre-commercial checklist
- No unauthorised monetary action or cross-user profile read.
- Payment callbacks are verified/idempotent; refunds and reconciliation tested.
- Visitor monetisation cannot be enabled by manipulating browser state.
- Artist IDs and certificate provenance are genuine and durable.
- Gallery minimum artist count and paid access enforced.
- Artwork dimensions/media, address protection, shipping quotes and tracking verified.
- Accessibility/mobile checks, data retention, terms/privacy and reporting channel ready.
- Backup/restore, monitoring, rollback and spending budgets agreed.

## Rollback and communications
Code rollback needs a previously validated release; avoid destructive schema changes and pause paid activity during monetary incidents. Preserve ledger/audit data. Tell affected users actual impact and remediation, never claim refunds or deliveries before confirmation.
